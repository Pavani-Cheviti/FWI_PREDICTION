🔥 What is the FWI?

The Forest Fire Weather Index (FWI) combines weather and fuel conditions to estimate fire behavior.

🌡️ Temperature

Higher temperatures increase evaporation and fire risk.

💧 Relative Humidity

Lower humidity means drier fuels and increased fire potential.

🌬️ Wind Speed

Strong winds can rapidly spread fires and make them harder to control.

🌧️ Rainfall

Recent rain reduces immediate fire danger.

🌿 FFMC

Fine Fuel Moisture Code — ease of ignition indicator.

🌳 DMC

Duff Moisture Code — indicates fuel availability.

🏜️ DC

Drought Code — shows long-term dryness.

🌪️ ISI

Initial Spread Index — combines wind and FFMC.

📈 BUI

Buildup Index — total fuel available for combustion.

🔥 Risk Levels
🟢 Low: FWI < 5.0
🟡 Moderate: 5.0 – 16.0
🟠 High: 16.0 – 31.0
🔴 Extreme: > 31.0
